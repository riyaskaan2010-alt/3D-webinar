Yes! We can prepare sugar-free, vegan, gluten-free, and nut-free options. Please mention your dietary requirements when placing an order, and we will ensure your treat meets your needs without compromising on taste.

We deliver across Madurai city and surrounding areas. Delivery is free on orders above Rs.500. For orders below Rs.500, a flat delivery charge of Rs.50 applies. Same-day delivery is available for orders placed before 2 PM.

All our products are freshly baked every morning using premium organic ingredients. We use traditional fermentation methods and avoid artificial preservatives. Every batch is carefully inspected for quality before delivery.

Orders can be modified up to 24 hours before delivery. For cancellations, please contact us immediately. Cancellations made more than 48 hours before delivery incur no charge; later cancellations may have a handling fee.

We accept cash on delivery, Google Pay, Paytm, PhonePe, bank transfers, and card payments. For online orders, payment confirmation ensures faster processing and delivery scheduling.
